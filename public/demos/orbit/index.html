    Repair guides

    Four screws, in this order.

    Every guide is on this page rather than behind a login, because a repair guide you cannot find is not a repair guide.

    Replace the battery 8 minutes
    1. Unplug the hub and wait thirty seconds for the ring to fade.
    2. Loosen the four M3 screws in the base. They are captive, so they will not fall out.
    3. Lift the cap, speaker and mainboard off as one stack. Set it face up.
    4. Squeeze the two clips on the battery tray and lift it clear.
    5. Drop in the new tray, refit the stack, and tighten to 0.4 Nm.
    Replace the speaker 6 minutes
    1. Unplug the hub. Loosen the four base screws.
    2. Lift the cap off. The speaker is the clay coloured layer underneath.
    3. Unclip the two pin connector at the rear left. Pull the plug, not the wire.
    4. Fit the new module, reconnect, refit the cap.
    Replace the mainboard 12 minutes
    1. Export your rules first: hold the ring for five seconds and it writes them to the USB port.
    2. Unplug and open as above, removing cap, speaker and battery tray.
    3. Release the four board standoffs by hand. No tool is needed.
    4. Fit the new board, reassemble, then restore rules from the same USB stick.
    Clean the microphone grille 2 minutes
    1. Leave the hub assembled. Switch the microphone off at the side.
    2. Brush the grille with a dry toothbrush, away from the ring.
    3. Do not use compressed air. It pushes dust into the acoustic mesh.
