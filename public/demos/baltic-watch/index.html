    Where the numbers come from.

    Nothing here is smoothed for presentation. If an instrument is out, the row says so rather than filling the gap.

    1. 01 Measure

      Each station carries a pressure sensor, an acoustic wave gauge, a two metre anemometer mast and a pair of thermistors. Everything samples continuously and averages to ten minutes.

    2. 02 Check

      Readings pass a range test, a step test and a neighbour test against the two nearest stations. A failed reading is flagged, not deleted, and stays flagged in the archive.

    3. 03 Model

      Three wave models run on the same wind field, at 1 km, 3 km and 9 km. The spread between them is what the confidence column shows.

    4. 04 Publish

      Observations go out ten minutes after the hour, forecasts four times a day. The whole archive is open and it goes back to 1994.
