Anatomy / 02

Everything on the outside
has a reason to be there.

  1. 01

    Sealed transport

    The tape path sits behind a gasketed window. Sand, spray and studio dust stay on the outside where they belong.

  2. 02

    Analogue VU, not a bar graph

    A real moving coil meter with a 300 ms ballistic response. You read level with your peripheral vision, not your attention.

  3. 03

    Keys you can find in the dark

    Five mechanical keys, each a different height and travel. Record is offset and stiffer on purpose.

  4. 04

    One dial for everything

    Input gain, monitor level and bias trim live under a single detented dial. Push to change what it controls.

  5. 05

    Ports on the edge, not the back

    Two XLR inputs on the top edge so cables fall away from the operator. Everything is on the outside of the seal.
