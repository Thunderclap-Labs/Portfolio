A bouldering gym in the old textile hall on Drobes. Four metres of wall, no ropes, no belaying and no waiting for a partner. Half the gym is stripped and reset every second Tuesday, so nothing you have already climbed stays up long enough to get boring.

    House rules

    1. Do not sit under a problem somebody is on.
    2. Brush the holds you have just greased.
    3. Down climb where you can. The mat is not a landing zone.
    4. Chalk balls only. Loose chalk stays in the bag.
    5. If a hold spins, tell the desk rather than the internet.
