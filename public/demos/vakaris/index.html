    The workshop 03

    We are not fast. A lamp takes eleven weeks from the first drawing to the box, and most of that is waiting: for brass to arrive, for glass to be blown and annealed, for lacquer to cure properly rather than nearly. Nothing is speeded up with heat.

    Everything electrical is done to the same standard whether it shows or not. Fabric flex, a switch rated for a hundred thousand throws, a plug you can rewire with one screwdriver, and an earth on anything with metal you can touch.

    We do not sell bulbs. Every lamp is designed around a 2200 kelvin filament because that is the colour the evening actually is here in December, and we would rather tell you what to buy than sell you a worse one.
